# Benchmark Success Formula — Metal Room Divider Design Guide

> Synthetic extraction across 21 dimensions (15 mandatory + 5 optional + Prose Craft).

## Structural dimensions
1. Opens with a design problem scene (not a product claim).
2. Early material comparison table.
3. Case-study-driven proof in the middle third.
4. Specification checklist near the end.
5. Closing section addresses maintenance/longevity.

## Voice dimensions
6. Confident but not salesy.
7. Uses designer vocabulary (spec, patina, mil).
8. Opinion intensity target: 6/10, with stronger judgments reserved for decision sections rather than spread evenly across every major section.

## Evidence dimensions
9. Factory/process photos.
10. Named project examples.
11. Material standards or certifications.

## Optional dimensions
12. Cost-range guidance.
13. Installation timeline.
14. Sustainability notes.
15. Regional code considerations.

## Prose Craft
21. Sentence variation: short punch followed by explanatory sentence.
