[su_spoiler title="Can a 4-meter laser-cut metal room divider be installed without cutting into existing marble flooring?" open="no"]
**Yes — by using a top-hung structural header beam paired with a concealed 3M VHB + compression silicone bottom shoe instead of core-drilled floor pins.** This keeps lateral deflection under 2.0 mm while preserving stone slabs and underfloor heating loops.
