# Skeleton — Metal Room Divider Design Guide

## Numeric Control Vector

- Imitation / Injection / Differentiation: 65 / 25 / 10
- AIO capture target: 4 natural answer moments
- Bold judgment target: 3
- Opinion intensity: 6/10
- Evidence pressure: 70/100
- Target H2 count: 7
- Format repetition tolerance: 2
- Rationale: the benchmark structure fits the article intent closely, but the project-specific material and installation proof must carry the buyer's decision sections.

## Section Contracts

1. **Opening scene** — designer problem, no pitch.
2. **What a metal divider actually does** — define function + benefits.
3. **Stainless steel grade comparison** — Grade 304 (dry interiors) vs Grade 316 (coastal/wet zones) exposure matrix.
4. **Design decisions** — pattern, scale, finish, mounting.
5. **Project case** — Toronto co-working install case.
6. **Specification checklist** — actionable.
7. **Maintenance & longevity** — realistic expectations.
8. **Closing judgment** — when to choose metal over glass/wood.
