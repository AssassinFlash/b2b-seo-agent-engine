### On-grid Inverters — the no-battery path
- **R5 Series:** 0.7 kW – 12 kW, single-phase & three-phase residential.
- **R6 Series:** 15 kW – 50 kW, three-phase C&I. R6-15k~25k publishes 98.6% max efficiency, 180–1000 V MPPT, 2 × 32 A string inputs, export limitation option.
- **C6 Series:** 75 kW – 125 kW, three-phase large C&I / utility-scale.

### Hybrid Inverters — the battery-ready path
- **H1 Series:** 3 kW – 6 kW, single-phase residential (legacy/current platform).
- **H2 Series:** 3 kW – 30 kW, single-phase & three-phase, including low-voltage options.
  - H2-3k~6k single phase: 97.8% max efficiency, 90–500 V MPPT, 90–520 V LiFePO4 battery range, <20 ms ATS switchover, IP65, 5-year warranty extendable to 20 years.
  - H2 can run in grid-tied mode without a battery; battery port stays inactive until a compatible battery is connected.

### All-in-One Energy Storage Systems — the battery-now path
- **HS2 Series:** 3 kW – 10 kW, budget/residential tower, expandable 5 → 30 kWh.
- **HS3 Series:** 3 kW – 12 kW, single-phase & three-phase, 6-in-1 integrated ESS.
  - HS3-3k~6k single phase: 93% round-trip efficiency, next-gen LFP, 6,000+ cycles at 80% DoD, <20 ms ATS, IP65, 10-year warranty.
- **CHS2 Series:** 30 kW – 50 kW, three-phase C&I cabinet, expandable 100 → 800 kWh.

### Smart Accessories
- Data Collector, eManager, Elekeeper cloud/EMS platform.

## Freshness anchors (verified)

- **NEC 2023:** Articles 690/705 govern grid-tied solar; Article 706 is added when a battery is installed. Source: NFPA 70-2023.
- **IRA residential clean energy credit:** 30% through 2032 for solar and standalone storage ≥3 kWh; steps down in 2033–2034 and expires for residential after 2035 as currently written. Source: IRS Form 5695 / IRC §25D.
- **EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542):** battery passport mandatory for industrial/EV batteries ≥2 kWh from February 2027. Source: EUR-Lex.
- **Net Zero Industry Act:** targets 40% of strategic net-zero technologies manufactured in the EU by 2030. Source: European Commission.

> All tax/regulatory claims must be framed as "as of 2026 / confirm with a professional," not individualized advice.
