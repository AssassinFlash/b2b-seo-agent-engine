## Hybrid inverters (battery-ready path)

### H2-3k~6k Single Phase Hybrid Inverter
- 3–6 kW AC output, single-phase.
- 90–500 V MPPT, 2 MPPTs.
- 97.8% maximum efficiency.
- Battery: external LiFePO4, 90–520 V high-voltage range, CAN/RS485 BMS.
- EPS backup: ATS switchover <20 ms, critical-load panel, generator auto-start input.
- Communication: CAN, RS485, RS232, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, 4G, PLC, DRM.
- Warranty: 5 years (extendable up to 20 years).
- IP65 enclosure.

## All-in-One ESS (battery-now path)

### HS3-3k~6k Single Phase All-in-One Energy Storage System
- 3–6 kW AC output, single-phase.
- 90–550 V MPPT, 2 MPPTs.
- All-in-one hybrid ESS with integrated PCS, BMS, EMS, and battery in a plug-in cabinet.
- Next-gen LFP cells, 6,000+ cycles at 80% DoD, modular expansion.

- EPS backup: <20 ms ATS switchover.

- Communication: CAN, RS485, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, 4G, DRM, Bluetooth.
