# SERP / Competitor Content Research

> Research window: 2026-06-23  
> Purpose: Map what currently ranks for the primary keyword cluster and identify the content gap Sunriver can occupy.  
> Primary keywords: `solar charger without battery`, `solar system without battery`, `solar panel without battery`, `home battery backup without solar`.

## SERP landscape summary

- **AI Overview coverage:** 100% of target keywords trigger a Google AI Overview.
- **Dominant page types:**
  1. Generic homeowner explainers (EnergySage, SolarReviews, Solar.com, Aforenergy).
  2. Product-specific pages from inverter/battery brands (FranklinWH, Briggs & Stratton, Sunchees, Energyscape Renewables).
  3. Reddit / DIY Solar Forum threads with anecdotal owner experiences.
- **Common content shape:** "Can solar work without a battery? Yes, here's how grid-tied works..." followed by a basic pros/cons list.
- **Gap:** No top-ranking page combines (a) a strong contrarian editorial voice, (b) manufacturer-informed technical depth, and (c) a clear "battery-ready" product decision path.

## Reader questions the SERP answers poorly

1. **"If I buy a hybrid inverter without a battery, what exactly do I lose and keep?"**
   - Most pages say "no backup" but don't explain the daylight-blackout paradox or the ~$85/year idle cost.
2. **"How much more will it cost to add a battery later vs. now?"**
   - Generic ranges only; no side-by-side TCO table.
3. **"Which inverter should I buy if I'm battery-curious but battery-shy today?"**
   - Brands push their own box; neutral guidance is weak.
4. **"Do I need a battery for the tax credit?"**
   - Often answered incorrectly or not at all.
5. **"What if I never add the battery?"**
   - Usually buried or ignored.
