# Research Summary — Sunriver Electric

> Consolidated from `/profile-research-deep` deliverables in `research-deep/` on 2026-06-23.

## What we confirmed

1. **Product evidence is sufficient.** Live crawl of Sunriver's site yielded verified specs for H2 hybrid inverters, HS3 all-in-one ESS, and R6 on-grid inverters. No internal assets are available, but public evidence supports the first article.
2. **The counter-intent angle is viable.** All four primary keywords trigger AI Overviews; KD is low (5–10); no existing Sunriver article owns this space.
3. **Competitor content is generic.** Top-ranking pages are either plain explainers (EnergySage, Solar.com) or brand product pitches. None combine contrarian voice, manufacturer depth, and a decision framework.
4. **Regulatory anchors are fact-checkable.** NEC 2023, IRA 30% credit step-down, EU Battery Regulation passport (Feb 2027), and Net Zero Industry Act can all be used with proper attribution and disclaimer.
5. **Style mechanics are extractable.** Five techniques from 饼干哥哥AGI / 颜Sir说 can be adapted: conclude-first, not-X-but-Y reframes, protagonist + number, claim-evidence-action, aphorism + artifact.

## What remains uncertain (assumptions)

- Exact Sunriver pricing and regional availability must be requested per quote; article should not invent prices.
- Real customer case studies are not available; protagonist stories must be anonymized composites or clearly hypothetical.
- Some product specs are from marketing pages; warranty/compliance claims should defer to official datasheets.

## Red lines for the first article

- No individualized tax or legal advice.
- No claims that battery-less solar avoids all permits or codes.
- No pricing without a published source.
- No duplication of existing Sunriver blog articles.
