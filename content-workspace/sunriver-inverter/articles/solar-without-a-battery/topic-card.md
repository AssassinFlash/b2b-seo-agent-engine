## Outline Sketch

1. **Opening:** Direct answer — yes, you can go solar without a battery, but the hardware you choose determines whether you pay twice later.
2. **The battery industry's hidden assumption:** Why most guides push batteries and what they leave out.
3. **Three hardware paths:**
   - On-grid inverter (R6) — cheapest, no backup, no expansion.
   - Hybrid inverter without battery (H2) — mid-cost, battery-ready, future-proof.
   - All-in-one ESS (HS3) — battery-now, highest cost, full backup.
4. **When no-battery makes sense:** Budget priority, grid-stable region, future expansion planned.
5. **When battery-now makes sense:** Frequent outages, time-of-use arbitrage, incentive windows.
6. **Regulatory / incentive anchors:** NEC, IRA, EU Battery Regulation (with disclaimers).
7. **Decision framework:** 4-question flow chart.
8. **Conclusion:** Choose the path that avoids the retrofit trap.
