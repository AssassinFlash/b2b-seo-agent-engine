NEM 3.0 Takeaway: Under Net Metering, the grid acts as a free, 100% efficient battery. Under Net Billing, exporting solar without a battery loses 75% of its value. Installing a SAJ H2 battery-ready inverter lets you store that surplus locally, avoiding the export trap and saving the full retail value.
