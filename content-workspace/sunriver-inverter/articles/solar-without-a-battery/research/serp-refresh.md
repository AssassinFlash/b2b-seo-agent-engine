## Executive Summary

1. **Solar.com is now the dominant #1 across the core “no battery” queries.** In the original benchmark research (2026-06-23) Solar.com was listed as a generic homeowner guide; today it holds the top organic spot for `"solar without a battery"`, `"do I need a battery with solar panels"`, and `"solar panels no battery"`.
2. **EcoFlow’s AU blog has entered strongly** for `"solar without a battery"` and `"solar panels no battery"` — a brand-product content play that did not appear in the original benchmark list.
3. **Aforenergy remains present** as a manufacturer/educational hybrid page, especially for `"solar without a battery"` / `"solar panels no battery"`.
4. **EnergySage has shifted down / become more query-specific:** it is still the canonical result for `"can I add battery to solar later"` (retrofit intent) but is less visible for the direct "no battery" question.
5. **The `"hybrid inverter without battery"` SERP is heavily product/manufacturer + forum driven** (Xindun, Enertech, Amazon, Reddit r/SolarDIY, SRNE, MuscleGrid). This is a more technical/B2B-heavy result set than the homeowner explainers that dominate the other four queries.
6. **AIO / featured-snippet presence is high** across all five queries. Direct "Yes/No" answers, comparison tables, and FAQ blocks are consistently returned by search tools.
7. **No evidence of SolarReviews, FranklinWH, Briggs & Stratton, or Sunchees** in the refreshed top results for these exact queries — they appear to have dropped or are ranking for adjacent terms not captured here.

## Implications for the Sunriver Article

1. **Benchmark refresh recommendation:** Solar.com is now the closest direct competitor for the "no battery" angle, not EnergySage. EnergySage remains the benchmark for the retrofit/add-later angle.
2. **Differentiation opportunity:** The SERP is crowded with generic "Yes, you can" answers. The Sunriver article can differentiate by focusing on the **manufacturer-informed product decision** (R6 on-grid vs H2 hybrid without battery vs HS3 battery-now) rather than repeating net-metering basics.
3. **AIO targets:** Ensure the draft captures direct-answer blocks for:
   - "Can you use solar without a battery?"
   - "Can a hybrid inverter work without a battery?"
   - "Can you add a battery later?"
   - "What is the difference between AC-coupled and DC-coupled battery retrofits?"
4. **Technical depth:** The `"hybrid inverter without battery"` SERP rewards product-specific technical detail (BMS protocols, PV-only mode, transfer time, battery voltage ranges). This supports Sunriver’s plan to cite SAJ H2 / R6 / HS3 specs.
5. **Forum insight:** Reddit/DIY owner experiences about "backup without batteries" and "retrofit regrets" remain a source of authentic reader questions and can feed the contrarian/rant-with-lessons voice.
