## Recommended *New* First-Article Angles (Excludes Existing Content)

### Option A — Solar Battery Charger Guide (Traffic + AIO)
- **Primary keyword:** `solar battery charger` (8,100/mo, KD 16)
- **Cluster reach:** ~15,200/mo across 12v chargers, car/boat chargers, trickle chargers
- **Why it wins:** Highest volume-to-KD ratio in the entire dataset; not covered at all; every major variant triggers AIO.
- **Article promise:** "Solar Battery Charger: How to Pick the Right One Without Overcharging Your Wallet"
- **Product connection:** Moderate — Sunriver sells inverters/ESS, not standalone chargers. Good for top-of-funnel traffic + internal links to H1/H2/HS2.

### Option B — Solar Inverter Types & Selection Guide (Category Anchor)
- **Primary keyword:** `solar inverter` (74,000/mo, KD 38)

- **Article promise:** "Solar Inverter Types: How to Choose the Right One for Your Project"
- **Product connection:** Strong — direct path to R5/R6/C6/H1/H2 product pages.

### Option C — Solar Battery Installation Guide (Commercial Intent)
