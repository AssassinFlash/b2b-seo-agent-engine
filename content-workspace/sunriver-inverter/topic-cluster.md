## 1. Executive Summary: A Strategic Content Roadmap

This topic pool is designed to establish Sunriver Electric as the most trusted, manufacturer-informed resource for homeowners navigating the inverter + storage buying process. The strategy is built on **4 Content Pillars** that align with Sunriver's SAJ-focused product lines and the strongest unowned keyword gaps.

### The 4 Content Pillars:

1. **Pillar 1: Counter-Intent Solar Education** — Address the questions the battery industry would rather homeowners did not ask. These are low-KD, high-AIO, emotionally sticky topics where Sunriver's on-grid and battery-ready hybrid products are the natural answer.
2. **Pillar 2: Battery-Ready Hardware Strategy** — Teach homeowners how to buy hardware today that does not lock them out of storage tomorrow. Positions H1/H2 hybrid inverters and HS2/HS3/CHS2 as future-proofing tools.
3. **Pillar 3: Product Selection & Decision Frameworks** — Build the definitive buyer guides for inverters, batteries, and all-in-one kits that the SERP currently lacks at a technical depth homeowners can actually use.
4. **Pillar 4: Cost, ROI & Regulatory Freshness** — Own the high-CPC, high-intent conversations around cost, payback, tax incentives, and code compliance.

After analyzing 537 broad-match keywords and cross-referencing Sunriver's existing 17 blog posts, **17 high-opportunity topics** are documented below. The first article, **'Solar Without a Battery,'** is locked because it combines the lowest competition, highest AIO coverage, and strongest product fit.

## 2. Benchmark Analysis Summary

- **Direct SERP competitors (EnergySage, SolarReviews, Solar.com):** These sites dominate generic homeowner queries with thin, brand-agnostic explainers. Their content is accurate but rarely manufacturer-informed, and almost never addresses the 'retrofit trap' or battery-ready hardware path.
- **Product competitors (FranklinWH, Tesla, Enphase, Huawei):** Produce polished product pages and technical PDFs, but few publish educational content that maps a homeowner's decision journey across multiple product categories.
- **Best-in-class content archetypes to replicate:**
  - **Contrarian explainers** that open with a scene and a surprising claim, then back it with data.
  - **Decision frameworks** that use comparison tables and 'if X, then Y' routing.
  - **Cost guides** that explain what drives price without giving fake fixed numbers.
- **Strategic gap:** No top-ranking page combines contrarian editorial voice, manufacturer-informed technical depth, and a clear battery-ready product decision path. Sunriver can own this gap.

## 6. AI Overview & Content Format Implications

Because AIO appears on almost every target keyword, every article in this queue should:

1. **Answer the core question in the first 150 words** with a definition + use-case frame.
2. **Use comparison tables** for 'vs' and 'best' queries.
3. **Include FAQ schema-eligible blocks** that mirror People Also Ask patterns.
4. **Cite primary sources** (SAJ datasheets, NEC/UL references, IRS/EU documents) so AIO engines trust the page.
5. **Maintain the contrarian, rant-with-lessons voice** established in the locked profile.

## 7. Content Gaps to Avoid

Do **not** chase these topics unless new evidence changes the calculation:

- **Generic 'solar panels' or 'solar energy' head terms** — too broad, winner-take-most SERP.
- **DIY installation guides** — Sunriver's role is supply partner, not installation labor.
- **Brand-vs-brand teardowns** — avoid direct competitor bashing; focus on decision frameworks.
- **Topics already well-covered on the Sunriver blog** unless a fresh angle adds significant value (e.g., hybrid inverter compatibility guide already exists; do not duplicate).
