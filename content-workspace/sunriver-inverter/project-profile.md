## Confirmed Business Model

- **Company / project:** Sunriver Electric
- **Domain:** https://sunriver-energy.example.com
- **Business model:** B2B supply partner and authorized distributor of SAJ solar inverters, hybrid inverters, and all-in-one energy storage systems (ESS).
- **Role in supply chain:** Serves distributors, installers, EPCs, and project buyers with product selection, documentation packs, and quote coordination.
- **Commercial goal:** Move buyers from product shortlist to technical review and pricing; supports global projects.

## Product / Service Scope

- **In scope:** SAJ-focused on-grid inverters (R5/R6/C6), hybrid inverters (H1/H2), all-in-one ESS (HS2/HS3/CHS2), and Elekeeper monitoring/EMS.
- **Out of scope:** Non-SAJ brands, bespoke engineering services, installation labor (Sunriver supplies hardware + docs).
- **Product categories:** Residential and C&I solar inverters; residential and C&I battery storage.
- **Key applications:** Grid-tied solar, battery-ready hybrid solar, backup power, self-consumption, peak shaving, EV charging integration.
- **Required proof:** Published product specs from Sunriver website; industry standards (NEC, IRA, EU Battery Regulation); competitor benchmark content.

## Target Buyers

- **Primary buyers:** Residential end-users / homeowners researching solar and storage options.
- **Secondary buyers:** Distributors / wholesalers comparing portfolios.
- **Tertiary buyers:** EPCs and installers matching inverter/battery specs to project scope.
- **Buying committee:** Homeowner + installer + sometimes utility/ AHJ; distributor procurement + technical review team.
- **Pain points:** Battery costs dominate quotes; fear of buying obsolete tech; confusion about whether a battery is mandatory; retrofit regret.
- **Decision criteria:** Upfront cost, future expandability, backup need, tax incentives, local grid codes, installer recommendation.

## Target Markets and Languages

- **Markets:** Global, with North America and Europe as leading contexts.
- **Languages:** English (first article).
- **Regional search behavior:** High-intent informational queries around "solar without battery," "hybrid inverter without battery," "home battery backup without solar."

## Brand Voice and Red Lines

- **Voice:** Conversational, contrarian, rant-with-lessons — inspired by 饼干哥哥AGI / 颜Sir说 — while remaining technically accurate and manufacturer-aligned.
- **Project role:** Sunriver is the knowledgeable supply partner that helps buyers avoid the "retrofit trap" and choose the right SAJ hardware path.
- **Claims allowed:**
  - SAJ H2 hybrid inverters can run without a battery in grid-tied mode.
  - H2 supports plug-and-play DC-coupled battery expansion when the buyer is ready.
  - R6 on-grid inverters are a cost-effective no-battery path.
  - HS3 is the all-in-one battery-now path.
  - Regulatory anchors as attributed, with disclaimers.
- **Claims forbidden:**
  - Battery-less solar avoids all permits/codes.
  - Any product works with any battery.
  - Specific pricing without a published source.
  - Tax advice presented as individualized recommendation.
  - "Zero electricity bill forever" or similar over-promises.
- **Tone boundaries:** No AI clichés; no generic "solar is the future" sermons; no paragraph that could appear on any competitor site.
- **Compliance boundaries:** All tax/regulatory claims framed as "as of 2026 / confirm with a licensed professional."

## Visual Design System & Asset Acquisition

- **Visual style:** Dark background, warm orange accents (`#ffb703` to `#fb8500`), technical/clean B2B aesthetic, flat diagrams over glossy 3D renders.
- **Typography:** Use monospaced labels for specs (`Space Mono` when available) and clean sans-serif for body labels.
- **AI generation rule:** **Image-to-image only.** Any AI-generated visual must start from a real product photo or real install photo. Pure text-to-image product visuals are not allowed.
- **Preferred image-to-image tool:** nanobanana (img2img mode) when available; alternatives allowed only if they preserve the real base image and do not invent product interfaces, ports, or labels.
- **Preferred search channels:** official Sunriver / SAJ pages first, then B2B platforms (Alibaba, Made-in-China, Amazon), then social/documentation/video sources (YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook) via Apify or equivalent tooling.
- **Denoise requirement:** short brand/model strings (SAJ, H2, HS3) need keyword-bag denoising and volume caps during search to avoid unrelated noise.
- **Format judgment:** use static WebP for small-text technical diagrams, GIF for energy-flow or slider interactions, interactive HTML only when the reader genuinely needs to manipulate the visual, and markdown tables when the structure is already clear.
- **Project visual library:** maintain a project-level `visual-library/` so sourced/generated assets are reusable and incremental across articles.
